Grocery Inventory Analysis & Dashboard

Excel-based analysis that optimizes replenishment, quantify risk, and surface supplier performance using ABC, stock-risk, valuation, and reorder logic. Built on a Kaggle dataset and finished with an executive dashboard.

Objective

Build an end-to-end inventory analytics workflow that answers: What should we reorder? Where is cash tied up? What’s at risk? and Which suppliers drive or endanger availability? The deliverable is a single-screen dashboard backed by pivot logic and clean Excel modeling.

Dataset

  • Source: Kaggle – Grocery Inventory
  • Key columns: Product_Name, Category, Supplier_Name, Warehouse_Location, Status, Product_ID, Supplier_ID, Date_Received, Last_Order_Date, Expiration_Date, Stock_Quantity, Reorder_Level, Reorder_Quantity, Unit_Price, Sales_Volume, Inventory_Turnover_Rate, percentage.
  • Helper columns added: Sales Value = Sales_Volume × Unit_Price; Inventory Value = Stock_Quantity × Unit_Price.

Analysis & Methods

  • Reorder Recommendations — 121 unique products; Different Product_ID's are different accross suppliers; Each Product_ID × Warehouse has distinct needs. Added Reorder_Flag (Stock_Quantity < Reorder_Level) with conditional formatting.
  • Stock Risk Analysis — Flagged Deadstock where Stock_Quantity ≥ Reorder_Level and Inventory_Turnover_Rate ≤ 5. Built pivots to aggregate Stock at Risk (qty) and Value of Inventory by Category/Supplier/Warehouse with drill-through.
  • ABC Analysis — Ranked products by Sales Value, computed % of items, cumulative % revenue, and classified: A (≤80%), B (≤95%), C (>95%). Delivered a Pareto chart and a summary table of item counts and revenue share by class.
  • Inventory Valuation — Quantified cash tied up via Inventory Value. Pivots by Category, Supplier, and Warehouse; surfaced Total Inventory Value, Top-3 categories, top supplier, and Deadstock value & %.
  • Supplier Performance — Reliability and exposure: Top suppliers by Inventory and Sales Value; % Backordered and % Discontinued per supplier to spotlight risk and negotiate improvements.

Dashboard

KPIs:

  • Inventory Value
  • % Products to Reorder
  • % Deadstock
  • A-Class Revenue
  • Riskiest Supplier

Visuals:

  • Top-10 Riskiest Suppliers
  • Deadstock Value by Category
  • Revenue by Grade (A/B/C)
  • ABC Pareto
  • Riskiest Supplier
  • Inventory Value by Category

Key Insights

  • A-class items (≈20% of SKUs) contribute ≈80% of revenue — focus replenishment and service levels here.
  • Deadstock pockets cluster in specific categories/suppliers — discount or rationalize to free working capital.
  • Supplier reliability varies; a few vendors drive a disproportionate share of backorders/discontinuations.
  • Warehouse-level splits reveal where cash is concentrated and where stock health is weakest.

Impact

  • Actionable reorder list at the SKU × Warehouse grain reduces stockouts without over-ordering.
  • Visibility into at-risk and idle inventory helps reclaim capital and shelf space.
  • Supplier scorecards enable better contracts and escalation with data.

How I Built It

  • Power Query for cleaning/typing dates and merging files; Tables + structured refs; PivotTables & PivotCharts; helper columns; conditional formatting; one-page dashboard layout with slicers (Category, Supplier, Warehouse, ABC, Status).
  • Design language: consistent color palette, KPI cards, Pareto cutoffs (80/95%), compact Top-N charts.

Potential Improvements

  • Simple demand forecasting (FORECAST.ETS) to make time-phased reorder suggestions.
  • Automated refresh via Power Query + OneDrive.
  • Supplier lead-time/OTIF tracking using Date_Received vs Last_Order_Date.
